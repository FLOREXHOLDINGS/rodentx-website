Home / Wildlife Removal / Mice & Rats

Mice & Rats.
The flagship.

The name is the promise. RodentX does full rodent programs — entry, harborage, removal, seal, monitor — for homes and businesses across the Finger Lakes.

Signs

  • Scratching or scurrying in walls, ceilings, or attic — especially at night
  • Droppings along baseboards, in cabinets, or near food storage
  • Gnaw marks on wood, wiring, food packaging, or insulation
  • Grease rub marks along runs, nesting material, or odor

What we do

  • Full entry-point survey — foundation, doors, vents, roofline, utility penetrations
  • Removal matched to the building and the infestation
  • Harborage reduction guidance — what to clear, seal, and store differently
  • Monitoring until activity stops

What we seal

Foundation gaps, door sweeps, vent covers, pipe and wire penetrations, garage and sill gaps, roofline openings. Every seal is listed in writing — what was sealed, and with what.

Where it applies: Homes, rentals, restaurants, warehouses, wineries & breweries, and other commercial buildings across the Finger Lakes Region.

Home / Wildlife Removal / Bats

Bats in Attic.
Careful. Lawful.

Bats in attics and wall voids need a season-aware, exclusion-first approach. We inspect, explain timing, and seal — without shortcuts.

Signs

  • Odor or staining near roost entries, droppings (guano) in attic
  • Activity at dusk near roofline, vents, or soffits
  • Chirping or rustling in wall voids or attic at dawn / dusk

What we do

  • Inspection + roost-entry identification
  • Exclusion-first approach — one-way exits where appropriate, then sealing
  • Cleanout and disinfection options for affected attic areas

Timing restrictions apply. Bat work has timing restrictions that are explained before work. We will tell you what can be done now, what must wait, and why — before any work begins.

What we seal

Roofline gaps, soffit and fascia openings, vent and chimney penetrations, siding and trim gaps. Sealed entries are documented in writing.

Home / Wildlife Removal / Raccoons

Raccoons in Attic.
Remove. Clean. Seal.

Raccoons are heavy, destructive attic invaders — torn vents, damaged insulation, contaminated spaces. We remove, restore, and seal the building so they stay out.

Signs

  • Heavy thumping or movement in attic, especially at night
  • Torn roof vents, soffits, or fascia; entry holes in roofline
  • Damaged or flattened insulation, odor, droppings

What we do

  • Removal + entry identification
  • Cleanout + disinfection options for affected areas
  • Insulation removal / installation options where needed

What we seal

Roof vents, soffits, fascia, chimney and vent penetrations, roofline gaps — with durable, animal-proof materials, documented in writing.
