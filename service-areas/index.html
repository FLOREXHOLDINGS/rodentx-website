Shaped by Its Lakes.

Finger Lakes Region, NY only. Five hubs plus the Corning–Elmira edge, one standard. Keuka is home — Penn Yan / Yates County.

Hubs

  • Penn Yan / Yates County — Home base, Keuka Lake. Includes Penn Yan, Dundee, Naples.
  • Rochester — Western Finger Lakes hub. Includes Rochester area.
  • Canandaigua–Geneva — Lakes corridor hub. Includes Canandaigua, Geneva, Seneca Lake.
  • Syracuse — Eastern hub. Includes Syracuse area, Auburn.
  • Ithaca — Southern hub. Includes Ithaca area, Cayuga Lake.

The Corning–Elmira edge — Southern Tier edge of the region. Includes Elmira, Corning, Painted Post.
