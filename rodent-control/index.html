Mice Removal &
Rodent Control.
Exclusion-first.

RodentX runs full rodent programs — entry survey, removal, sealing, and monitoring — for homes and businesses across the Finger Lakes. Exclusion-first: we seal the building, not just treat the symptoms.

Signs

  • Scratching or scurrying in walls, ceilings, or attic — especially at night
  • Droppings along baseboards, in cabinets, or near food storage
  • Gnaw marks on wood, wiring, food packaging, or insulation
  • Grease rub marks along runs, nesting material, or odor

What we do

  • Full entry-point survey — foundation, doors, vents, roofline, utility penetrations
  • Removal matched to the building and the infestation
  • Harborage reduction guidance — what to clear, seal, and store differently
  • Monitoring until activity stops

What we seal

Foundation gaps, door sweeps, vent covers, pipe and wire penetrations, garage and sill gaps, roofline openings. Every seal is listed in writing — what was sealed, and with what.

Where it applies: Homes, rentals, restaurants, warehouses, wineries & breweries, and other commercial buildings across the Finger Lakes Region.
